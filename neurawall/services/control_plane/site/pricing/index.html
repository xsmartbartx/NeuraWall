Pricing questions.

Where do I pay and cancel?

In the NEXORA console, in one place. Cancel there at any time.

What does "your own Anthropic key" mean?

On Pro, Claude calls use your own Anthropic account, so Anthropic bills you for them directly. NeuraWall meters the calls but does not cap them.

What happens if I run out of Claude budget on Business?

The offline advisor takes over until the period resets. Detection and enforcement are not affected.
